# 📖 Glossary & Definitions

Terms used throughout the Server Rules and Organization Rules, defined in one place. Where a term already has its own rule page, this entry gives the short version and links to the full rule.

---

## Fail RP (FRP)

Roleplay that ignores realism, immersion, or the situation your character is actually in — behavior no real person would exhibit. Examples: casually chatting while held at gunpoint, ignoring injuries, refusing to react to danger, or sitting in a compound all day waiting to be breached (see [Compound Rules, O.7.2.2](./organization-rules/compound-rules.md)).

## Fear RP / Value of Life

The expectation that your character reacts to danger the way a real person would. If you are outnumbered, out-gunned, or a firearm is pointed at you at close range, you must comply with reasonable demands rather than fight back or flee carelessly. Referenced in [Robbery Rules](./server-rules/robbery-rules.md), [Vehicle Standards](./server-rules/vehicle-standards.md), [Compound Rules, O.7.2.1](./organization-rules/compound-rules.md), and [Demands & Payouts, O.11.1](./organization-rules/demands-and-payouts.md).

## Powergaming

Forcing an action or outcome onto another player without giving them a chance to respond or resist in RP. Example: `/me disarms and cuffs [player]` with no opportunity for the other player to react.

## Metagaming

Using out-of-character (OOC) information — Discord, streams, external voice chat, a previous character's memory — for in-character advantage. See [Character & Communication, 1.3.7](./server-rules/character-and-communication.md).

## RDM (Random Deathmatch)

Killing or attacking another player without a valid initiation or RP justification. All combat must be preceded by a valid **Initiation** (see below) unless a specific rule says otherwise, such as inside a Red Zone (see [Zone Rules, 1.7.1](./server-rules/zone-rules.md)).

## VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch)

Using a vehicle as a weapon — intentionally running someone over or ramming them — without valid RP justification. Always prohibited regardless of context. See [Final Conduct, 2.3.2](./server-rules/final-conduct.md).

## Initiation

A clear, in-character action or statement that establishes hostile intent and gives the other player a chance to react before force is used — for example, a verbal demand. Warning shots do not count as a valid initiation.

A valid initiation must be preceded by at least 5 minutes of genuine player interaction — you cannot initiate on someone out of nowhere for no reason. Combat, robbery, or hostage-taking without a valid initiation is treated as RDM.

## Combat Logging

Disconnecting, menu-restarting, or otherwise leaving the game to avoid the outcome of an active fight or RP scenario. See [Combat Rules, 1.9.1](./server-rules/combat-rules.md).

## NLR (New Life Rule)

Governs what your character remembers and can act on after going Dead Dead or being revived from Last Stand. See [New Life Rule, 1.2](./server-rules/new-life-rule.md) and [Organization NLR, O.10](./organization-rules/organization-nlr.md).

## Dead Dead / Last Stand

The two death states used throughout the rules. **Last Stand** is a downed, incapacitated state you can be revived from. **Dead Dead** is a full death that requires a hospital respawn. Memory and revenge rules differ between the two — see [New Life Rule, 1.2](./server-rules/new-life-rule.md).

## Hard-PK (Hard Permanent Kill)

A staff-authorized permanent removal of a character from ongoing RP. Used as an in-fiction consequence for severe scenarios, such as a hostage's crew refusing reasonable demands during a kidnapping (see [Demands & Payouts, O.11.1](./organization-rules/demands-and-payouts.md)). Only valid when authorized by staff — not something a player or org can declare unilaterally.

## Witch Hunting

Repeated, targeted harassment of a specific player or organization through OOC channels (Discord, social media, etc.) instead of in-character conflict or proper staff reporting. See [Reporting & Witch Hunting, O.13.2](./organization-rules/reporting-and-witch-hunting.md).

## Green Zone / Red Zone

Defined safe and PVP areas that govern where combat, robbery, and hostage RP are allowed. See [Zone Rules, 1.7](./server-rules/zone-rules.md) for the full list and rules.
