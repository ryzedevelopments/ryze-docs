# 🧠 1.2 New Life Rule

## New Life Protocol

- 1.2.1 If you go Dead Dead (bleed out / respawn), you forget how, why, and where the incident occurred — and everything else that happened in the 30 minutes leading up to your death
- 1.2.2 If you're revived from Last Stand — by EMS or anyone else — you keep your memory of the incident; the forgetting rule does not apply
- 1.2.3 You may not seek revenge on anyone involved in an incident you're required to forget, unless you've been reminded in character per 1.2.4
- 1.2.4 A surviving member who witnessed the event may relay it to you in character — once properly reminded this way, you may act on that information, including seeking revenge
