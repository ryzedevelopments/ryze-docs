# 👀 O.6 Scouting Rules

## Scout Standards

Scouts exist to gather information and support roleplay progression, not to artificially inflate numbers during hostilities.

---

## O.6.1 Untagged Scouts

Untagged associates may act as Scouts during hostility, subject to the eligibility rules in [Membership & Tagging, O.2.1](./membership-and-tagging.md) (maximum of 1 Scout; violating this results in a 2-week cooldown for the untagged member).

- Scouts may observe and relay information

### Scouts may NOT:

- Participate in shootouts
- Hold hostages
- Provide direct combat assistance
- Carry out hostile actions

---

## O.6.2 Tagged Members Acting as Scouts

Tagged members acting in a scouting capacity remain bound by all hostile RP rules.

Scouting should enhance roleplay and intelligence gathering. It must never be used to circumvent hostile caps or create unfair advantages.

:::warning
Any attempt to abuse scout mechanics for combat stacking may result in staff action.
:::
