# 🚨 O.12 Strike System

## Ryze RP — Strike System

The Strike System exists to hold orgs accountable through immersive, in-city consequences, not instant removals. Strikes scale based on severity, and punishments are enforced in character whenever possible. Staff only steps in directly when a situation requires it.

Strikes impact an org's money, time, reputation, and progression in the city.

---

## O.12.1 Minor Strikes

Minor Strikes are issued for low-level issues that harm the RP environment or show poor org conduct.

### Examples include:

- Low-effort or lack of meaningful roleplay
- Poor behaviour during org conflicts
- Disrespectful or negative interactions with PD or civilians
- Any behaviour that damages immersion or breaks server rules

Minor Strikes are issued at staff discretion — this list does not cover every scenario.

:::info
A pattern counts too: if several members of the same org rack up multiple rule-breaking instances within a short timeframe, that pattern can be issued as a Minor Strike against the org, even if no single instance alone would qualify.
:::

### O.12.1.1 Minor Strike Fees

Each Minor Strike adds an in-city financial penalty:

| Strike           | Fee        |
| ---------------- | ---------- |
| 1st Minor Strike | $3,000,000 |
| 2nd Minor Strike | $5,000,000 |
| 3rd Minor Strike | $8,000,000 |

These fees must be paid in character and represent the org's loss of stability, resources, and influence.

---

## O.12.2 Major Strikes

Major Strikes are issued for serious rule violations, regardless of how many Minor Strikes an org has.

### Examples include:

- A member of the org caught cheating or exploiting (aimbot, ESP, macros, exploits, etc.) — cheating is a zero-tolerance issue, and orgs are expected to vet and police their own roster
- Other severe rule violations at staff discretion

### O.12.2.1 Major Strike Consequences

Each Major Strike results in:

- Tier demotion (org drops one tier — see [Organization Tiers, O.3](./organization-tiers.md))
- Potential bans for the member(s) directly involved, at staff discretion

### O.12.2.2 Disbandment

:::danger
Major Strikes stack fast and disbandment is not far off — three (3) at any time results in the organization being disbanded, however they were earned. Cheating incidents count immediately, with no grace period: an org that keeps recruiting or protecting cheaters is on a short path to disbandment, not a guaranteed three strikes.
:::
